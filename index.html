A quiet counter for a practice you repeat.

Mantras, prostrations, breaths, pages, reps. Tap to count, and watch the days accumulate into something you can see.

Mindful Repetitions is an iPhone and iPad app for people who keep a daily practice and want an honest record of it — without a feed, a streak that shames you, or an account to create.

  • Tap to count One large target, built to be used with your eyes closed. Counts are recorded per day, and a day you enter by hand counts the same as a day you tapped.
  • Private by design No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party code of any kind. The developer never receives your data and could not read it if asked.
  • Syncs through your own iCloud Your practices appear on your other devices through your private iCloud database. Turn iCloud off and the app keeps working on the device alone.
  • Yours to take with you Export everything you have recorded as a JSON file, whenever you want, for any reason. Export and import are never restricted.
  • Reminders that stay on your phone Practice reminders are local notifications scheduled by iOS. Nothing is sent to a server, and nothing leaves your device.

Pricing

Mindful Repetitions is free for 14 days, with every feature open. If you want to keep using it after that, you can continue with a monthly or annual subscription, or buy the app outright with a one-time purchase.

The free trial begins a subscription. Unless you cancel before it ends, it renews automatically at the price shown in the App Store, and continues until cancelled. You can cancel any time from Settings on your device — the current prices for your country are always the ones shown on the App Store listing and on the app's purchase screen.
