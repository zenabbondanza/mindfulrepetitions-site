A quiet space for a practice you repeat.

Mantras, prostrations, breaths, pages, reps. Tap to count, and watch the days accumulate into something you can see.

Mindful Repetitions is an iPhone and iPad app for people who keep a daily practice and want an honest record of it — without a feed, a streak that shames you, or an account to create.

  • Tap to count One large target, built to be used with your eyes closed. Counts are recorded per day, and a day you enter by hand counts the same as a day you tapped.
  • See the practice over time Calendars, goals, groups and statistics make long-term progress visible — without turning a missed day into a punishment.
  • Dedicate what you practise Attach a dedication to what you repeat, and watch it take shape through the Journey, Tree Ring, Stack and Grow views.
  • Private by design Your practice lives in your own private iCloud account — no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party code of any kind. The developer never receives your data and could not read it if asked.
  • Syncs through your own iCloud Your practices appear on your other devices through your private iCloud database. Turn iCloud off and the app keeps working on the device alone.
  • Yours to take with you Export your practices, counts, groups, goals, dedications, reminder settings and synced appearance settings as a JSON backup, whenever you want. Export and import are never restricted.
  • Reminders that stay on your phone Practice reminders are local notifications scheduled by iOS. Nothing is sent to a server, and nothing leaves your device.

Pricing

There are three ways to unlock Mindful Repetitions — Monthly, Annual, or a One-Time Unlock. Every paid option opens exactly the same features.

Eligible new subscribers may be offered a 14-day free trial on a subscription. Apple determines who is eligible, and the app's purchase screen always shows the exact offer and renewal price available to you.

Starting a free trial starts an auto-renewable subscription. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, it renews at the price and on the date shown by the App Store, and continues until cancelled. One-Time Unlock is a separate purchase — buying it does not cancel or refund an active subscription.

If paid access ends

Nothing is deleted. You can keep viewing your records, and importing or exporting backups, for as long as you like. Recording, editing, restoring, deleting, reminders and appearance changes need an active subscription or the One-Time Unlock.
