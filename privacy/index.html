Privacy Policy

Effective 23 September 2026

Mindful Repetitions is a habit and repetition tracking app for iPhone. This policy explains what the app stores, where it is kept, and who can see it.

The short version: your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud account. The developer never receives it. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

What the app stores

Everything you enter, including:

  • the names of the practices you track, and the groups you organise them into
  • your daily counts and their dates
  • goals, including their names, targets and deadlines
  • dedications — the personal notes or affirmations you attach to a day
  • unit labels, colours, themes and other appearance settings
  • reminder times and any custom reminder text you write
  • optionally, a background image, if you choose one from your photo library

Where it is stored

On your device. All of the above is saved locally on the iPhone or iPad you are using.

In your iCloud account, if iCloud is available. So your practices appear on your other devices, the app syncs through Apple's CloudKit using your private iCloud database. This is your personal iCloud storage. The developer has no access to it and cannot read, export or recover anything stored there. If you sign out of iCloud or disable it, the app continues to work using only local storage.

Data stored on your device may also be included in your regular device backups, which are managed by Apple or by you, not by this app.

What the developer receives

Nothing.

There is no account system, no sign-up, no email collection, and no server operated by the developer. There is no analytics package, no crash-reporting service, no advertising SDK, and no third-party code that collects information.

The app connects only to Apple services used for iCloud sync and App Store purchases. It does not send your app data to the developer or to third-party services. The developer cannot see your practices, your counts, your notes, how often you use the app, or whether you use it at all.

Photos

If you choose a background image, the app uses Apple's system photo picker. That picker runs outside the app: you select one image, and only that image is handed over. The app has no access to the rest of your photo library and never requests it.

The app keeps two copies of what you chose: the original image, and the cropped version it displays. The original is kept so you can re-frame the crop later without going back to your photo library.

Both files are saved on that device only. They are not uploaded anywhere and are not synced to your other devices.

Notifications

Practice reminders are local notifications, scheduled and delivered by iOS on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, and no notification content leaves your device.

You can turn reminders off at any time, per practice or all at once, in the app's settings or in the iOS Settings app.

Purchases

Mindful Repetitions is free to use for a trial period, with every feature available. After the trial, continued use requires a paid plan: a monthly or annual subscription, or a one-time purchase that unlocks the app permanently. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled, and can be managed or cancelled at any time in the App Store.

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details, card number, or billing address — only the confirmation from Apple that a purchase or subscription exists for your Apple Account, which the app uses to unlock it. Restoring a purchase on another device is handled by Apple in the same way.

Device identification

To avoid mixing two people's data if a device changes iCloud accounts, the app stores a one-way cryptographic hash (SHA-256) derived from your iCloud account identifier. The identifier itself is never stored, and the hash is never transmitted anywhere — it stays on your device and is used only to notice that the account has changed.

Your data is yours

  • Export: at any time you can export all of your data as a JSON file. Where that file goes and who sees it is entirely up to you.
  • Delete: deleting a practice moves it to Recently Deleted, where it stays for 30 days and can be restored, after which it is permanently removed. Deleting the app removes all of its local data from the device.
  • Delete from iCloud: data synced to iCloud can be removed through iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, or by deleting the app on all your devices.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It has no accounts, no messaging, no user-to-user features, and no advertising.
