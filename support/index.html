Common questions

My practices aren't showing up on my other device

Both devices must be signed in to the same iCloud account, with iCloud Drive turned on, and both need a network connection. Syncing happens in the background through Apple's CloudKit, so a change made offline appears on your other device once both are online again — usually within a minute, occasionally longer if iCloud is busy.

If a device has been offline for a long time, open the app and leave it in the foreground for a moment; that is when it reconciles.

I paid already, but the app is asking me to buy again

Open the purchase screen (Unlock Full Access) and tap Restore Purchases. This asks Apple what your Apple Account owns and re-applies it.

The purchase is tied to the Apple Account that bought it, not to the device. If you have more than one Apple Account, make sure the device is signed in to the one you used at the time of purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by the app. On your device open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Mindful Repetitions, and cancel there. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the period you already paid for ends.

If you cancel during the free trial before it ends, you are not charged.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and all refunds — the developer cannot issue one. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I get my data out?

In the app's settings, choose Export Data. You get a JSON file containing everything you have recorded, which you can save anywhere you like. Export and import are never restricted — they work whether or not you have paid.

I deleted a practice by mistake

Deleted practices go to Recently Deleted and stay there for 30 days, with their history intact. Restore it from there any time within that window. After 30 days it is removed permanently and cannot be recovered.

How do I turn reminders off?

Reminders can be switched off for one practice in that practice's settings, or all at once with the master reminder toggle. You can also turn off notifications for the whole app in Settings → Notifications → Mindful Repetitions on your device.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. Your practices, counts and history stay exactly where they are, on your device and in your iCloud account, and Export Data keeps working so you can always take a copy with you.

Do you collect any data about me?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code in the app at all. Your data lives on your device and in your own private iCloud database, and the developer has no way to read it. The privacy policy sets this out in full.
